What we do

The full detailing playbook

Everything that makes a car look, feel and smell brand new, done by hand and in the right order.

Hand Wash & Dry

Foam pre-soak, two-bucket wash and a lint-free microfiber dry. No tunnels, no brushes, no swirl marks.

Clay & Decontamination

Bonded grit, tar and iron fallout come off so the paint feels glass-smooth before we polish.

Machine Polish

Dual-action polishing lifts light swirls and haze and brings back deep, wet-look gloss.

Wax & Sealant

A slick protective layer that beads water, resists grime and keeps the shine rolling.

Interior Deep Clean

Vacuum, steam and agitation for carpets, mats, seats, door cards and every seam in between.

Leather Care

Gentle cleaner, then conditioner to keep leather soft, even-toned and protected from cracking.

Wheels, Tires & Glass

Barrels, faces and calipers scrubbed. Tires dressed. Glass left invisible.

Finishing Touches

Trim restored, jambs wiped, vents cleaned and a final walk-around under inspection lights.

How it works

From dull to dialed in

  1. 01

    Inspect

    We walk the car with you, check paint and interior condition, and agree on the plan.

  2. 02

    Wash & Decon

    Foam, hand wash, clay and wheels. The car comes back to a clean slate.

  3. 03

    Correct & Protect

    Machine polish for gloss, then wax or sealant to lock it in.

  4. 04

    Final Inspection

    Interior reset, glass and trim, then a last check under lights before handover.
